# Apple Cultivation in India: Primary-Source Agronomy, Fertilizer Calculator & Orchard Reference (Passes 1–4)

## Executive Summary: 10 Non-Negotiable Facts for Indian Apple Growers
1. **Plant clean, virus-indexed material:** Mosaic, Apple Stem Pitting, and rootstock pathogens silently stunt young orchards; India's **Clean Plant Programme (NHB)** is hazard-indexing **535 apple samples** across J&K, HP, and Uttarakhand (`NHB / CPP 2025`).
2. **Match rootstock to soil drainage (Stop Collar Rot Mortality):** **Never** plant **MM.106** in heavy clayey or poorly drained soils—it is highly susceptible to *Phytophthora cactorum* collar rot, whereas **M.9** demands deep, irrigated, well-drained loam and staking (`HP Horticulture PoP`; `DergiPark 2025`).
3. **Never replant apple straight after uprooting old apple without treating Apple Replant Disease (ARD):** Fungi, *Pratylenchus*/*Xiphinema* nematodes, and soil fatigue stunt young replacements unless pits undergo solarization, bio-consortia (AMF/*Trichoderma*/*Pseudomonas*), and virgin topsoil replacement (`SKUAST-K 2024`; `HP PoP`).
4. **Plant 25–33% compatible diploid pollinizers + place beehives (Zero sprays in bloom!):** Without synchronized diploid pollinizers (`Golden Delicious`, `Granny Smith`, `Redlum Gala`, `Manchurian` crabapple) and bees (`4–6 colonies/ha`), blossoms drop unpollinated (`SKUAST-J`; `Temperate Fruit Crops`).
5. **Respect chilling hours (`<7°C`) by elevation:** Standard Delicious cultivars need **800–1,500 chilling hours** (`1,500–2,700 m` AMSL), whereas warm lower belts (`400–1,000 m`) require low-chill **HRMN-99 (`300–500` hrs)**, **Anna**, or **Dorsett Golden** (`NHB`; `NIF 2025`).
6. **Prevent White Root Rot (*Dematophora necatrix*) with drainage & mounded beds:** Peak mortality occurs during June–September monsoon waterlogging; keep a `60 cm` undisturbed basin around trunks and cut off stagnant hillside runoff (`ICAR-CITH 2022`; `HortiKashmir 2026`).
7. **Seal every pruning cut `>2.5 cm` immediately:** Unsealed stub cuts allow *Botryosphaeria*/*Diplodia* canker and dieback to kill limbs (`"dead-like plants"`); coat cuts immediately with **Copper oxychloride paste / Bordeaux paste** (or Chaubattia paste—prefer lead-free copper paste) (`ICAR`; `SKUAST-K`).
8. **Stop indiscriminate heading cuts that shade the inner canopy:** Excessive upper-tier vigour shades lower spurs, ruins flower-bud differentiation, and drives **alternate bearing ("on-year / off-year")** (`Tall Spindle Review`; `HP PoP`).
9. **Quit spraying blindly (Cut chemical burn & Red Mite flares):** Only ~25% of surveyed Kashmir growers followed SKUAST-K schedules while spending `Rs 97,360–1,09,870/ha` on pesticides (`Nov 2026 survey`); calendar spraying kills predatory mites and triggers European Red Mite outbreaks (`SKUAST-K`).
10. **Verify hill land units before dosing:** **1 Acre = `5.0` Himachal Bighas** (`809.37 m²` each) = **`8.0` J&K Kanals** (`505.86 m²` each) = **`20.16` Uttarakhand Nalis** (`200.67 m²` each) (`Land Units 2026`).

## 3. Commercial Cultivars, Pollinizers & Low-Chill Apples in India

> **Direct Answer (`apple varieties in india`, `pollinizer`):** Legacy Indian orchards rely heavily on **Royal/Red Delicious**, while modern HDP blocks plant high-color strains (**Scarlet Spur-II, Red Chief, Super Chief, Oregon Spur, Jeromine, Red Velox**) and **Gala** strains, paired with **25–33% compatible diploid pollinizers** (`Golden Delicious, Granny Smith, Redlum Gala, Fuji, Manchurian crabapple`) (`HP PoP`; `SKUAST-J`).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal / Red Delicious** | Standard vigorous; striped/blushed | `1,000–1,500 hrs` (`5,500–7,500 ft`) | Main (**needs pollinizer**) | Classic aroma; **severe alternate bearing**, weak color at lower altitudes |
| **Scarlet Spur-II / Red Chief / Super Chief** | Compact spur; dark red | `1,000–1,200 hrs` (`4,500–7,500 ft`) | Main (`needs pollinizer`) | Early color at `4,500–6,000 ft` (`HP PoP`); **small fruit on M.9 in poor soils** |
| **Oregon Spur / King Roat** | Semi-spur; dark red | `900–1,200 hrs` | Main | Compact fruiting spurs; needs `25–33%` pollinizers |
| **Jeromine / Red Velox / Red Cap Valtod** | Standard-vigour High-Color | `900–1,200 hrs` (`4,500–8,000 ft`) | Main | High pack-out at lower belts; turns overly dark above `7,500 ft` (`HP PoP`) |
| **Gale Gala / Redlum Gala / Gala Venus** | Standard; bright early red | `700–1,000 hrs` | **Dual: Main + Pollinizer (`P`)** | Early (`Jul–Aug`) export-grade; smaller at highest elevations (`HP PoP`) |
| **Golden Delicious (Reinders)** | Standard/semi-spur; yellow | `800–1,100 hrs` | **Classic Pollinizer (`P`)** | Reliable pollen donor; alternate bearing if unthinned, wet russeting |
| **Granny Smith / Sun Fuji** | Vigorous; green (`GS`) / pink (`Fuji`) | `700–1,000 hrs` (`long season`) | **Pollinizer (`P`) + Commercial** | `Granny Smith` needs long frost-free fall (`Oct–Nov`); `Fuji` long storage (`HP PoP`) |
| **Ambri (`J&K`) / Shireen & Firdous (`SKUAST-K`)** | Heritage (`Ambri`); Scab-resistant (`Shireen/Firdous`) | Temperate Kashmir | Main | `Ambri` long storage (`slow bearing`); `Shireen/Firdous` bred for **Scab resistance** (`CITH`) |

- **Fixing Unpollinated Flower Drop:** Maintain **`25–33%` diploid pollinizers** (`30–50%` in cold/rainy valleys; **avoid sterile triploids** like *Mutsu*/*Jonagold*), add flowering crabapples (`Manchurian`/`Snowdrift`), place **`4–6` beehives/ha (`2–3/ac`)** at 10–15% bloom, clear competing mustard weeds, and apply **ZERO sprays in full bloom** (`SKUAST-J`; `HortiKashmir 2026`).

## 4. Climate, Chilling Hours & Site Selection
> **Direct Answer:** Standard Delicious apples need **800–1,500 chilling hours (`<7°C`)** (`1,500–2,700 m` AMSL), `1,000–1,250 mm` rainfall, and frost-free bloom weather (`<-2°C` kills pistils) (`NHB`; `HP PoP`). Lower belts (`4,000–6,500 ft`) favor **North/North-East** slopes (avoiding winter sunscald canker) with high-color strains, while high belts (`6,500–8,000+ ft`) use **South-East** slopes (`HP PoP`). Anti-hail nets prevent hail stripping and moderate summer sunscald.

## 5. Orchard Soils, Waterlogging & Apple Replant Disease (ARD)
> **Direct Answer (`apple root rot`, `apple plants mortality`, `apple replant disease`):** Apple requires deep, well-drained loam (`pH 5.5–6.8`, `OC >1.0%`). Orchard mortality ("dead-like plants") stems chiefly from **White Root Rot (*Dematophora necatrix*)** in monsoon wet soils, **Collar Rot (*Phytophthora cactorum*)** on `MM.106` in clay, and untreated **Apple Replant Disease (ARD)** (`ICAR-CITH 2022`; `SKUAST-K 2024`).

- **White Root Rot vs. Collar Rot:** *Dematophora* coats rotted brown roots in **white cottony mycelium** (`Jun–Sep`); *Phytophthora* causes **slimy reddish-brown bark rot right at the soil collar line**. *Fix:* Cutoff hillside drainage ditches, raised mounds, keep **graft union `15–20 cm` above soil**, expose collar/roots (`Nov–Dec`), scrape dead tissue, paint **Copper oxychloride / Bordeaux paste**, and drench root zone (`Apr, Jun, Jul, Sep`) per **ICAR/SAU** protocol (`Carbendazim + Mancozeb` or *Trichoderma + Pseudomonas* `10 g/L`, `Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb` for Collar Rot) (`ICAR`; `HP PoP`).
- **ARD Rescue (`SKUAST-K 2024`; `HP PoP`):** Shift new pits to old alleyways, fallow with mustard/marigold or solarize soil, fill with virgin topsoil + decomposed FYM + **AMF (VAM) + *Trichoderma* + *Pseudomonas***, and use Geneva (`G.11/G.41/G.214`) rootstocks where available.

## 7. Canopy Architecture & Pruning Science
> **Direct Answer (`apple pruning practices`):** Bad pruning—heavy heading cuts, upper-tier shading, narrow `<30°` forks, and unsealed stubs—causes upright sterile shoots, *Botryosphaeria* canker dieback, weak lower buds, and alternate bearing. Use **Tall Spindle / Modified Leader thinning cuts**, remove limbs `>50%` trunk diameter via beveled renewal cuts, bend limbs `45°–60°` (`>90°` in Tall Spindle), prune `15 cm` below dead cankers with sterilized tools (`70%` ethanol), and immediately coat all cuts `>2.5 cm` with **Copper oxychloride / Bordeaux paste** (`or lead-free Chaubattia alternative`) (`ICAR`; `Tall Spindle Review`; `SKUAST-K`). Add light summer pruning (`July–Aug`) of upright water-sprouts for natural color.

## 8. Irrigation, Moisture Stress & Fertigation
> **Direct Answer (`apple orchard irrigation`, `apple fertigation`):** Despite `1,000–1,250 mm` annual rain, dry spells in **May–June (cell division)** and **August–September (sizing)** slash apple size and trigger Bitter Pit. Drip on `M.9` HDP supplies **2–4 L/tree/day** in early spring, **6–10 L/tree/day** in May–June, and **10–15+ L/tree/day** in July–August, saving **40–50% water** (`ICAR-CITH`; `SKUAST-K`).

- **Mulching:** Maintain a `10–15 cm` organic mulch ring (`30 cm` clear of trunk) to cut evaporation `30%+`, keep feeder roots below `28°C`, and curb soil-splash collar rot (`ICAR-CITH`; `HP PoP`). Irregular drought-then-flood irrigation triggers fruit cracking.

## 9. Nutrient Management, Foliar Nutrition & Breaking Alternate Bearing

> **Direct Answer (`apple fertilizer schedule`, `apple fruit size`, `alternate bearing in apple`):** Standard bearing apple trees (`10+ yrs`) take **`700 g N : 350 g P₂O₅ : 700 g K₂O + 70–100 kg FYM/tree/yr`** (`Dr. YSP UHF Nauni`; `SKUAST-K`), while mature **`M.9` HDP trees** take **`120–180 g N : 50–80 g P₂O₅ : 150–250 g K₂O/tree/yr`** via weekly drip (`ICAR-CITH`; `YSP UHF`). Breaking uneven cropping and small size requires **thinning fruitlets within `10–25 days` post-petal fall**, **Oct post-harvest Urea+Zn+B foliar sprays**, **Pink-Bud Boron**, and **4–6 Calcium sprays**.

### State / Institute NPK Baselines
| Institution / System | Tree Age / Rootstock | `N` (`g/tree/yr`) | `P₂O₅` (`g/tree/yr`) | `K₂O` (`g/tree/yr`) | FYM (`kg/tree/yr`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dr. YSP UHF Nauni (HP)** | Standard (`1–9 yrs`) | `70 x Age` | `35 x Age` | `70 x Age` | `10 x Age` |
| **YSP UHF / SKUAST-K** | **Standard Bearing (`10+ yrs`)** | **`700`** | **`350`** | **`700`** *(to `1,000` heavy crop)* | **`70–100`** |
| **Uttarakhand (`Chaubattia`)** | Standard Bearing (`10+ yrs`) | `500–700` | `250–350` | `500–700` | `50–100` |
| **YSP UHF Nauni (HDP)** | **`M.9` Drip (`1–7+ yrs`, `14–15` splits)** | `35 x Age` (`Yr 5 = 175`) | `17.5 x Age` (`Yr 5 = 87.5`) | `35 x Age` (`Yr 5 = 175`) | `5–20` |
| **SKUAST-K / CITH (HDP)** | Fertile Karewa `M.9` ceiling watch | `45–120 kg N/ha` total (`trim N if shoots >50 cm`) | `40–60 kg/ha` | `120–180 kg/ha` | `15–20/tree` |

*(Acidic Soil Rule: When `pH < 5.5`, use **CAN** or **Dolomite/Lime** rather than acidifying Ammonium Sulphate alone.)*

### Critical Annual Split Windows
1. **Late Autumn (`Nov–Dec`):** `100%` FYM + `100%` `P₂O₅` (`SSP`) + `50–100%` `K₂O` (`MOP`) in feeder ring `30 cm` inside dripline (`never touching trunk`).
2. **Pre-Bloom (`Feb–Mar`, `2–3 wks` pre-budbreak):** `50–67%` of `N` (`Urea` / `CAN`).
3. **Post-Petal Fall (`Apr–May`):** Remaining `33–50%` `N` + balance `K` **ONLY if fruit set is heavy**! Cut/skip post-bloom N by `50–100%` in an **"off-year"** (`prevents wild shoot growth`).
4. **Fruit Sizing (`Jun–Aug`):** Zero heavy soil N; `SOP (0:0:50)` + foliar **Ca**.
5. **Post-Harvest Leaf Fall (`Oct–Nov`):** **`3–5% Urea` (`30–50 g/L`) + `0.5% ZnSO₄` + `0.1–0.2% Solubor`** on still-green leaves before drop (`reloads spring spur buds` + `decomposes scabbed leaves`) (`SKUAST-K`).

### Foliar Secondary & Micronutrients
- **Boron (`Pink Bud` & `Oct`):** **`0.1–0.15% Solubor/Boric Acid` (`1–1.5 g/L`)**—fixes pollen-tube growth & unpollinated flower drop (*do not exceed `0.2%`!*) (`SKUAST-K`; `YSP UHF`).
- **Calcium (`4–6 sprays`, `15d` gap from `2–3 wks` post-petal fall):** **`0.3–0.5% CaCl₂` (`3–5 g/L`)**—prevents Bitter Pit & Cork Spot (*skip if `>28–30°C`*) (`YSP UHF`).
- **Zinc (`Delayed Dormant` / `Oct`):** **`0.5% ZnSO₄` (`5 g/L`) + `0.25%` lime**—cures Little Leaf/rosettes (`SKUAST-K`).
- **Magnesium (`Walnut stage`):** **`1–2% MgSO₄` (`10–20 g/L`)**—prevents premature yellow leaf drop; **K:** `1% SOP` (`10 g/L`) for size/color (`CITH`).

### Breaking Alternate Bearing & Cutting Chemical Over-Dependency
- **Fruit Thinning (`10–25 days` post-petal fall, `8–12 mm`):** Hand-thin to **1 king fruit/cluster** spaced `15–20 cm` apart (`30–40` leaves/fruit), OR use SAU/CIB&RC-approved **NAA (`10–15 ppm`)** / **6-BA (`75–100 ppm`)**—*avoid **Carbaryl** due to bee toxicity/regulatory bans!* Breaks seed-GA suppression of next year's buds AND doubles fruit size (`SKUAST-K`; `HP PoP`).
- **Stop Calendar Spraying & WSF/PGR Misuse:** Unnecessary calendar insecticides kill predatory *Typhlodromus* mites (exploding European Red Mites and inflating `Rs 1L/ha` bills); excess *Promalin*/`Ethephon` on stressed trees worsens fruit drop; WSF poured onto waterlogged/acidic soils is wasted. **Never mix `CaCl₂`/`Ca(NO₃)₂` with Sulphates (`SOP`, `MgSO₄`, `ZnSO₄`) or Phosphates (`MAP`, `MKP`, `19:19:19`)** (`Tank A/B` separation).
- **Biostimulants (Neutral Synthesis):** **AMF + *Trichoderma* + *Pseudomonas*** strongly reduce ARD mortality (`SKUAST-K 2024`); **Humic acids** improve fine-root aggregation in compacted soils alongside drainage/mulch (`cannot cure root rot!`); **Seaweed (*Ascophyllum*) + Amino acids** support cold-bloom fruit set and color alongside Boron/Calcium under spring weather swings (*Scientia Horticulturae*).

- `Dec–Feb`: Dormant pruning (`thin >50% limbs`), **paste cuts `>2.5 cm`**, drainage ditches, FYM+P+K.

## 12. Major Diseases: Photo Triage & Control

> **Direct Answer (`apple root rot`, `apple dieback`, `apple scab`, `marssonina blotch`):** Tree mortality stems from **White Root Rot (*Dematophora*)**, wet-soil **Collar Rot (*Phytophthora*)**, and unsealed **Canker/Dieback (*Botryosphaeria*)**, requiring drainage, root-collar exposure, dead-wood surgery, and Copper pastes (`ICAR-CITH`). Leaf-dropping **Apple Scab**, **Marssonina**, and **Alternaria** require Oct `5%` Urea sanitation plus **FRAC-rotated fungicides** (`SKUAST-K 2026`).

## 13. Fruit Drop, Fruit Size & Fruit Quality Failures
> **Direct Answer (`apple fruit drop`, `apple fruit size issues`, `apple fruit quality`):** Drop occurs in 3 waves: **Post-bloom** (pollinizer/Boron/bee failure), **June Drop** (May–Jun drought + over-set competition), and **Pre-harvest Drop** (ethylene/heat/mites). Fix via `25–33%` pollinizers + Pink-Bud Boron, May–Jun irrigation + pea-stage thinning (`30–40` leaves/fruit), `4–6` **Calcium (`CaCl₂`)** sprays, summer canopy pruning for natural red color, and **NAA (`10 ppm` = `1 ml Planofix / 4.5 L`)** `10–20 days` before expected pre-harvest drop (`YSP UHF`; `SKUAST-K`). *(Avoid over-using Ethephon!)*

## 14. Harvest, Grading & Post-Harvest Storage
- **Harvest Indices:** `130–145 DAFB` (Delicious) / `110–120` (Gala), **Starch-Iodine `2.5–3.5` (`1–6` scale)** for CA storage (`4–5` fresh), firmness `16–18 lb/sq in` (`NHB`).
- **Grading & CA Storage:** `Extra Large >80 mm`, `Large 75–80 mm`, `Medium 70–75 mm`, `Small 65–70 mm`, `Extra Small <65 mm`. Store at **`0–1°C`, `90–95% RH`, `1.5–2.0% O₂`, `1.5–2.0% CO₂`** for **6–9 months** (`NHB`).

## 17. Apple Fertilizer Calculator Logic, Formulas & Worked Examples

### Inputs & Formulas
- **Inputs:** System (`Standard Age-Scaled` vs `M.9 HDP`), Tree Age (`1–10+ yrs`), State PoP (`YSP UHF`, `SKUAST-K`, `Uttarakhand`, `Low-Chill`), Area & Unit (`Acre 4,046.86 m²`, `Hectare 10,000 m²`, `HP Bigha 809.37 m²`, `J&K Kanal 505.86 m²`, `UK Nali 200.67 m²`), Spacing or manual Tree Count, Crop Load (`Heavy/Normal` vs `Light Off-Year` vs `Excess Shoots >50 cm`), Soil Test (`pH, OC, N, P, K`), Irrigation (`Basin` vs `Drip`).
- **Base Dose:**
  - Standard: $\text{EffAge} = \min(\text{Age}, 10)$; `70:35:70 x EffAge` (`g/tree`) + `10 x EffAge` (`kg FYM/tree`).
  - `M.9` HDP (`YSP UHF` 50% rule): $\text{EffAge}_{\text{HDP}} = \min(\text{Age}, 7)$; `35:17.5:35 x EffAge_HDP` (`g/tree`) *(with SKUAST-K fertile Karewa `120 kg N/ha` ceiling warning)*.
- **Soil Test (`ICAR-IISS`):** `<280 N, <10 P, <118 K` = `+25%` (capped at `700:350:700` Standard); Medium = `0%`; `>560 N, >25 P, >280 K` = `-25%`.
- **Off-Year Multiplier (Post-Bloom N only):** `Light/Off-Year = 0.0 to 0.5`; `Excess Shoots = 0.5`.

### Worked Example (a): `5 Himachal Bighas` (`1.0 Acre`) `MM.111`/Standard (`Yr 8`, `6 x 6 m`, `YSP UHF Nauni`)
- **Soil Test:** `pH 5.3` (Acidic), `OC 1.1%` (High), `N 260` (Low `+25%`), `P 15` (Med `0%`), `K 105` (Low `+25%`), Heavy Bloom.
- **Population:** $4,046.86 \div 36 = \mathbf{112\text{ trees/acre}}$ (`~22.4/HP Bigha`).
- **Year-8 Adjusted Targets:** `N` = $560 \times 1.25 = 700\text{ g/tree} = \mathbf{78.40\text{ kg/ac}}$ | `P₂O₅` = $280\text{ g/tree} = \mathbf{31.36\text{ kg/ac}}$ | `K₂O` = $560 \times 1.25 = 700\text{ g/tree} = \mathbf{78.40\text{ kg/ac}}$ | `FYM` = **`8,960 kg`** (`~9.0 t`).
- **Products (`Acidic pH 5.3 -> CAN 25% N`):** **SSP (`16%`):** **`196.00 kg`** (`~4` bags `50 kg`, Nov–Dec) | **MOP (`60%`):** **`130.67 kg`** (`~2.6` bags, `65.3 kg` Nov–Dec + `65.3 kg` Apr–May) | **CAN (`25% N`):** **`313.60 kg`** (`~6.3` bags: **`188.16 kg`** Feb–Mar pre-bloom + **`125.44 kg`** Apr–May post-petal fall).
- *Auto-Alerts:* `pH 5.3 < 5.5` -> flag Dolomite/Lime check; schedule Pink-Bud Boron, `4–6` CaCl₂ sprays, and `Oct` Urea+Zn+B.

### Worked Example (b): `8 Kashmir Kanals` (`1.0 Acre`) `M.9` HDP (`Yr 5`, `3.0 x 1.5 m`, `ICAR-CITH / YSP UHF`)
- **Soil Test:** Medium (`0%`), `pH 6.6`, Normal Set.
- **Population:** $4,046.86 \div 4.5 = \mathbf{899\text{ trees/acre}}$ (`~112.4/Kanal`).
- **Year-5 HDP Totals (`175:87.5:175 g/tree`):** **`157.33 kg N`**, **`78.66 kg P₂O₅`**, **`157.33 kg K₂O`**, **`13,485 kg` FYM**.
- **Drip WSF (`15` weekly splits, `Mar 15–Jun end`):** **MAP (`12:61:00`):** **`128.95 kg`** (`supplies 15.47 kg N`) | **SOP (`0:0:50`):** **`314.66 kg`** | **Drip Urea (`46% N`):** $(157.33 - 15.47) \div 0.46 = \mathbf{308.39\text{ kg}}$.
- **Per-Week Injection:** **`8.60 kg MAP + 20.98 kg SOP + 20.56 kg Urea per acre/week`**.
- *Auto-Alert:* Monitor shoot length (`30–45 cm`); trim June N if shoots exceed `50 cm` (`SKUAST-K Karewa check`).

### Hard Validation Rules
1. **Off-Year N Brake:** Cut/zero post-bloom N when `crop_load = light_off_year`.
2. **Acidic Soil (`pH < 5.5`):** Switch Urea/AS recommendations to **CAN** + Dolomite/Lime notice.
3. **`M.9` Overdose Guard:** Cap `M.9` annual N (`<=245 g/tree`).
4. **Tank A / Tank B + `CaCl₂` Temp Rule:** Separate `Ca` from `SO₄`/`PO₄`; block `CaCl₂` sprays `>28–30°C`.
